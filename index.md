# Holiday Pay Support

Holiday Pay is a calculator for flight attendants that estimates holiday pay from your hourly rate, credited flight time, time away from base (TAFB) and holiday hours.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between "Enter Hours" and "Trip Times"?**
Enter Hours is for when you already know your TAFB and holiday hours. Trip Times works them out from your report time, release time and holiday dates.

**How are holiday hours counted in Trip Times?**
Holiday hours are the part of your trip that falls on the holiday's calendar days, in your iPhone's current time zone. If your contract uses domicile time and your phone is set to a different time zone, use Enter Hours instead.

**Can I enter hours as 48:30 or 48.5?**
Both work. The bubble next to each field shows the other format so you can check your entry.

**Is my data backed up or shared?**
No. Everything stays on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your saved trips.
