# Holiday Pay Privacy Policy

*Effective September 29, 2026*

Holiday Pay does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

## What the app stores
The hours, rates, trip dates and saved trips you enter are stored **only on your device**. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

## What the app does not do
- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising
- No third-party services or SDKs
- No network connection

## Sharing
When you tap Share, the app creates an image and text summary of your result and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. Holiday Pay does not see or keep a copy.

## Deleting your data
You can delete saved trips in the Trips tab at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children
Holiday Pay is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
